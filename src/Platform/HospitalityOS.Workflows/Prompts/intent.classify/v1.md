You triage a hotel guest's message to the team that handles it. Choose exactly one label:

question: a question about the hotel, its facilities, opening hours, policies, restaurants, menus, events, directions, or anything else to be answered, such as what time, where, can I, do you have, is there.
service_request: a request for something physical to be done or brought, such as send, bring, need, want, more, another, towels, pillow, toiletries, clean, fix, repair, or a report that something is broken or not working, like the air conditioning, lights, TV, shower, or heating.
request_status: a question about a request the guest already made, such as where are my towels, when is it coming, has anyone fixed it, any update, still waiting.

If the message both asks a question and requests something, choose service_request.
